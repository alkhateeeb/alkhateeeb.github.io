Our Story

From the Mountains of Jordan

قصة منحل الخطيب — من جبال الأردن إلى مائدتك

🏔️🐝🍯

Ajloun, Jordan · 2015

Every journey begins with a step — ours began with a single hive…

In 2015, our journey started far from the world of computers and programming — as a hobby and nothing more. As the days passed, our apiary began to grow and develop, and alongside the apiary grew our love for this passion.

What began as a personal hobby gradually became a family affair. I taught my wife and all my children this craft — both the science and the hands-on practice. And within us grew the values of patience, generosity, and teamwork that the bees themselves taught us.

By the grace of Allah, our apiaries have since spread across all regions of the Kingdom, so that we may bring you every variety of fine Jordanian honey.

Our Locations

Our Bee Yards

Each location produces a distinctly different honey — a true reflection of its unique flora, altitude, and microclimate.

🏔️

Northern Highlands

Wildflower meadows at 800–1,200 m elevation. Home to our Wild Flora and Centaurea varieties. The rich biodiversity here means each jar carries aromatic, delicious, distinctive flavours that change season to season.

🌿

Jordan Mountain Foothills

Eucalyptus, acacia, and mountain thorn trees line the quiet slopes of Jordan's highlands. The warm, humid microclimate produces our signature Eucalyptus Honey with its distinctive mentholated character and warm amber hue.

🌳

Southern Lowlands

The ancient sidr (jujube) trees of Jordan's southern lowlands yield our rarest harvest. Revered since antiquity, Sidr Honey from this region is deeply aromatic, thick, and exceptional in quality. We produce very limited quantities each year.

🌼

Seasonal Rotation Strategy

We rotate hive placements to follow each plant's flowering peak — moving hives so bees forage only from the target bloom during its prime. This is what makes our honey genuinely single-source, not just labelled that way.
